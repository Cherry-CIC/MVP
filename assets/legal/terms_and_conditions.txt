14. Buyer Protection

Purpose of Buyer Protection
cherry's Buyer Protection offers a refund policy if an item is lost, damaged, or significantly not as described (SNAD). Customer Support will assist you according to our Refund Policy. Payments are held in escrow until the transaction is completed. Please note that Buyer Protection is neither insurance nor a legal protection service and complements your existing legal rights. A mandatory Buyer Protection fee applies to every purchase made through cherry's "buy" button.

Refund Policy
You may request a refund if the item is:
Lost or damaged during shipping
Significantly not as described (SNAD): Includes differences in size, colour, or condition (e.g., stains, odours, or holes), missing items from a bundle, or counterfeit items.

To report an issue, click "I have an issue" in your message screen within 2 calendar days of delivery or notification of a lost item (the Refund Request Period). If an issue is not reported within this timeframe, the transaction will be marked as completed.

Handling SNAD Items

For non-counterfeit SNAD items:
Contact the seller to resolve the issue amicably.
If unresolved, escalate the issue to cherry within the Refund Request Period.

The seller can then:
Approve a refund without requiring the item to be returned.
Request a return within 5 business days. Return shipping costs are the buyer's responsibility unless agreed otherwise. Once returned, sellers have 2 days to confirm receipt or raise further issues.
For bundles, only the SNAD items need to be returned. The refund will be calculated for those SNAD items.
If sellers fail to act within 7 days, cherry's Customer Support will make the final decision.

For counterfeit items:
cherry investigates authenticity and may require additional information from both parties.
Outcomes may include refunding the buyer and cancelling the transaction without requiring a return.

Refund Ineligibility

You will not receive a refund if you:
Report an item as SNAD but it's determined not to be.
Confirm receipt of the item without issues.
Fail to report the issue within the Refund Request Period (7 days from delivery).
Alter, use, or wash the item before return.
Abuse the Buyer Protection policy.

Types of Refunds

Refunds may cover the following fees (refer to cherry's Refund Table for details):
Buyer Protection fees.
Item price.
Prepaid shipping fees.
Return shipping costs (in cases of lost or damaged parcels).
