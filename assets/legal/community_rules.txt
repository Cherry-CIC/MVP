Community Rules

Date last updated: 19.09.2026

cherry is a charity-first community built on trust. These Community Rules explain what you may share and sell, how to report concerns, and what we may do when the rules are broken. They form part of cherry's Terms and Conditions.

1. Acceptance and scope
You must accept these Community Rules and cherry's Terms and Conditions before creating, uploading or publishing user-generated content.

These Rules apply to listings, photographs, descriptions, usernames, profile information, messages, reviews and any other content you submit to cherry. You are responsible for your content and must make sure it follows these Rules and all applicable laws.

2. General standards
Content shared through cherry must be accurate, honest and relevant. It must respect other people's rights, safety and privacy.

You must not share content or behave in a way that is unlawful, fraudulent, threatening, abusive, discriminatory, sexually explicit, misleading or otherwise harmful.

3. Prohibited listings and goods
You must not list, advertise, request, buy or sell:

- Stolen goods or goods obtained through fraud or other criminal activity.
- Goods that you do not own or do not have the legal right to sell.
- Goods whose sale, possession, import or export is prohibited by law.
- Counterfeit goods, replicas, unauthorised copies or goods using a trade mark without permission.
- Weapons, ammunition, explosives or items intended to cause injury.
- Illegal drugs, controlled substances, drug paraphernalia or substances presented as having unlawful effects.
- Prescription medicines, unapproved medical products or products requiring professional supervision.
- Tobacco, nicotine products, vaping products, alcohol or other age-restricted goods.
- Hazardous, recalled, contaminated or unsafe goods.
- Human remains, bodily materials, live animals or products obtained through unlawful harm to animals.
- Identity documents, payment details, account credentials or another person's personal information.
- Sexual services or anything soliciting sexual activity in return for money, goods or another benefit.
- Items that infringe copyright, trade marks, design rights, patents or other intellectual-property rights.
- Anything that cherry, its payment providers or its delivery providers cannot lawfully support.

This list is not exhaustive. We may prohibit other items where reasonably necessary to protect users, charities, third parties or cherry.

4. Counterfeit goods
Counterfeit goods and unauthorised replicas are strictly prohibited. Calling an item a replica, dupe, lookalike, inspired by or similar does not make it acceptable.

You must not hide or misrepresent an item's brand, origin or authenticity. You must not use misleading photographs, packaging, receipts or authenticity documents. We may ask for reasonable evidence of ownership or authenticity and may remove a listing if it is not provided.

5. Intellectual property
Only upload content that you created or have permission to use. You must not copy another seller's photographs or description, misuse a person's name or image, or use copyrighted material, brands, logos or trade marks without permission.

By uploading content, you confirm that you own it or have all permissions needed to use it, that it does not infringe another person's rights, and that you have the legal right to offer the item for sale.

Rights holders may report suspected infringement through cherry's reporting or support channels. A report should identify the protected work or right, the relevant content, the reason for the report and the reporter's authority to act. We may restrict or remove reported content while we investigate and may ask the affected user for evidence.

6. Abuse and harmful content
You must not publish, send or encourage:

- Threats, harassment, intimidation, stalking or bullying.
- Violence, self-harm, suicide or harm to another person.
- Terrorism, violent extremism or criminal organisations.
- Hate speech or attacks based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, age or another protected characteristic.
- Content intended to humiliate, degrade or single someone out for abuse or ridicule.
- Graphic, gratuitous or sadistic violence.
- Dangerous acts likely to cause serious injury.
- Impersonation of another person, charity, organisation or cherry representative.
- Another person's private or confidential information without permission.
- Malicious links, malware, spam or repeated unsolicited advertising.
- Fraud, deception or exploitation of another user.

7. Sexual content and child safety
cherry does not permit pornography or sexually explicit content. You must not publish, request, send or promote:

- Pornography, explicit nudity or depictions of sexual activity.
- Content primarily intended for sexual gratification.
- Sexual services, sexual solicitation or sexual activity in return for money, goods or another benefit.
- Non-consensual intimate images, including manipulated or AI-generated images.
- Sexual threats, sexual harassment, exploitation, sextortion or predatory behaviour.
- Content that sexualises, exploits, abuses or endangers anyone under 18.
- Grooming, trafficking or attempts to obtain sexual material from anyone under 18.
- Child sexual abuse material, including computer-generated, illustrated or manipulated material.

Ordinary, non-sexual photographs showing clothing being worn may be allowed when they are relevant to the listing and are not exploitative, explicit or unnecessarily revealing.

We will remove content involving child sexual abuse or exploitation and report it to the appropriate authorities where required by law.

8. Fraud and misleading activity
You must not:

- Create false or misleading listings.
- Conceal important faults or misrepresent an item's condition, brand, origin or authenticity.
- Use another person's identity, payment details or account.
- Manipulate reviews, ratings, prices, searches or recommendations.
- Create or use additional accounts to avoid restrictions.
- Use cherry to arrange prohibited transactions elsewhere.
- Send phishing messages or request passwords, security codes or unnecessary financial information.
- Abuse payments, refunds, promotions, donations or buyer-protection processes.

9. Reporting and blocking
You can report content or users that may break these Rules using cherry's reporting or support channels. Reports should identify the relevant content or user and explain the concern. Reports involving immediate danger, child safety, credible threats, sexual exploitation or suspected serious criminal activity will be prioritised.

You may block another user using cherry's blocking controls where available. Blocking is separate from reporting. If you believe a user has broken these Rules, you should also report them.

Deliberately false, malicious or abusive reports are prohibited.

10. Moderation and enforcement
We may use proportionate automated checks, filters and human review to detect, investigate and address possible breaches. Publishing content does not mean that cherry has approved, authenticated or endorsed it.

If we reasonably believe that content or conduct breaks these Rules, the Terms and Conditions or the law, we may:

- Prevent content from being published, reduce its visibility or remove it.
- Issue a warning or request information or evidence.
- Restrict listing, messaging, purchasing or payment features.
- Cancel a listing or transaction where permitted by the Terms and Conditions.
- Temporarily suspend or permanently close an account.
- Prevent the creation of replacement accounts.
- Preserve relevant information as evidence.
- Notify affected users, rights holders, service providers or lawful authorities where appropriate.

We will consider the seriousness, frequency, context and potential harm of the conduct. A serious breach may result in immediate restriction or account closure without a prior warning. Where legally permitted, we will explain the main reason for a significant enforcement decision.

11. Appeals
You may challenge a content-removal, restriction, suspension or account-closure decision through cherry's support channels. Explain why you believe the decision was incorrect and provide any relevant evidence.

We will review the appeal and may uphold, change or reverse the original decision. This appeal process does not limit any legal rights you may have.

12. Changes to these Rules
We may update these Rules to reflect changes to cherry, applicable law or safety requirements. If a change materially affects what users may publish or how these Rules are enforced, we will provide reasonable notice and may require users to accept the updated Rules before creating or uploading more content.
