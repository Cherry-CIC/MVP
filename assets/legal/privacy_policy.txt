Privacy Policy

Date last updated: 24.10.2026

This policy explains what personal data cherry collects when you use our app and website, why we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. We have written it to be as clear and short as we can.

1. Who we are
cherry is run by CHERRY .T COMMUNITY INTEREST COMPANY, a community interest company registered in England and Wales with company number 16119941 ("cherry", "we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

This policy covers the cherry mobile app (the "App") and our website (the "Website"), together the "Platform". cherry is currently available in the United Kingdom only, and you must be 18 or over to create an account.

We follow the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

You can contact us about anything in this policy at cherry.community.interest.company@gmail.com.

2. The personal data we collect
We only collect what we need to run the Platform. We do not collect special category data (such as health, religion or ethnicity), we do not collect your precise location, and the App does not contain analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

2.1 Your account
- Email address, username, first name and password (passwords are stored securely by our authentication provider and we never see them).
- Your user ID and how you signed in (email, Google or Apple).
- An optional profile photo, which other members can see.
- A record of the version of our Terms and Conditions you accepted and when.

2.2 Buying and selling
- Listings you create, including photos, title, description, size, price and the charity you choose to support.
- Items you like.
- Your UK mobile number and saved delivery address.
- Order details, including the item, amount, delivery address or locker, order status and a payment reference.
- Shipping details, including your name, address, email, phone number, tracking number and shipping label.

2.3 Payments
Card payments are handled by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card details. We receive your email, user ID, postcode, the amounts paid and a payment reference.

2.4 Disputes, reports and blocks
- If you raise or respond to a dispute, the reason, your messages and any photo evidence you upload, plus notes from our team.
- If you report or block a member or listing, who made the report, what was reported, the reason, any evidence and the outcome.

2.5 Emails we send you
Order and shipping emails include your email address, first name, item details, order number and, for sellers, the shipping label.

2.6 Our website
- If you use our contact form, your name, preferred name, email, phone number, preferred contact method, message and whether you want development updates.
- Cookies and similar technologies, which may record your IP address, browser type and preferences (see section 9).

2.7 On your device
The App stores some basic profile information on your phone so it loads faster. This is removed when you sign out or delete the App.

3. Why we use your data and our lawful basis
UK GDPR requires us to have a lawful basis for each use of your data. These are the bases we rely on.

3.1 To provide the Platform (contract)
We use your account, listing, order, payment and shipping data to create and run your account, let you list, buy and sell items, take payments, arrange delivery and send you emails about your orders. This is necessary to perform our contract with you (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). If you do not provide this data, we cannot provide these services.

3.2 To keep cherry safe (legitimate interests and legal obligation)
We use dispute, report and block data, and limited technical logs, to resolve problems between members, moderate content, prevent fraud and misuse, and keep the Platform secure. We rely on our legitimate interest in running a safe marketplace (Article 6(1)(f)) and, where the Online Safety Act 2023 applies, our legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

3.3 To meet our legal obligations
We keep records of sales and payments because UK company and tax law requires us to (Article 6(1)(c)).

3.4 To answer your enquiries (legitimate interests)
We use the details you give us through our contact form or by email to reply to you.

3.5 Development updates and non-essential cookies (consent)
We only send development update emails and set non-essential website cookies if you have agreed. You can withdraw your consent at any time by unsubscribing or changing your cookie settings.

4. Who we share your data with
We never sell your personal data. The charities you support through cherry do not receive any buyer or seller details.

Other members can see your username, profile photo and listings. When you buy an item, the seller receives the information needed to send it to you.

We use the following service providers, who process data on our behalf under contracts that protect your data.
- Google Firebase and Google Cloud, for account sign-in, our database, file storage and our backend. Our backend runs in London.
- Google and Apple, if you choose to sign in with them. They share your name and email with us.
- Google Places, to help you find and fill in your address. The text you type is sent to Google.
- Stripe, to take and process payments.
- Sendcloud and InPost, to create shipping labels and deliver parcels.
- Resend, to send order and shipping emails.
- Framer, which hosts our website and its contact form.
- Google Gmail, which hosts the inbox that receives contact form messages and data protection requests.

We may also share data with the police, regulators or courts if the law requires us to, or to protect our members and the public.

If cherry is ever reorganised or merged with another organisation, your data may be transferred as part of that, and you would be told first.

5. International transfers
Some of our providers (including Google, Stripe and Resend) process data outside the UK, including in the European Economic Area and the United States. Where data leaves the UK, we make sure it is protected by UK adequacy regulations (including the UK-US data bridge for certified US companies) or by the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep your data
We keep personal data only for as long as we need it.
- Account, profile, likes and saved address are kept until you delete your account. If your account is inactive for 12 months, we will email you before deleting it.
- Unsold listings and their photos are deleted with your account. Sold listings are kept with the order record.
- Orders, payments and shipments are kept for six years from the end of the financial year they relate to, as UK law requires. If you delete your account, these records are anonymised so they are no longer linked to you.
- Disputes and member reports are kept for 12 months after they are resolved. Any order or payment record linked to a dispute follows the six year rule above.
- Your Terms acceptance record is kept for the life of your account and six years afterwards, so we can show what you agreed to.
- Website enquiries are kept for 12 months after our last contact with you.
- Development update consent is kept until you unsubscribe.
- Server logs are kept for up to 30 days.
- Internal order exports used by our team are deleted within 24 hours.

7. Deleting your account
You can delete your account at any time in the App from Profile, then Settings, then Delete account. You can also ask us to delete your account by emailing cherry.community.interest.company@gmail.com from the address linked to your account.

When you delete your account we remove your sign-in details, profile, profile photo, saved address, likes, unsold listings and their photos. We anonymise the records we must keep by law, such as orders and payments. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke cherry's access to your Apple ID.

8. Your rights
Under UK GDPR you have the right to
- ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
- ask us to correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
- ask us to delete your data;
- ask us to restrict how we use your data;
- object to us using your data where we rely on legitimate interests;
- ask for data you gave us in a portable format;
- withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

To use any of these rights, email cherry.community.interest.company@gmail.com. We will reply within one month. We may need to check your identity first. We do not charge a fee unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

9. Cookies on our website
Our website uses essential cookies so the site works and remembers your cookie choices. We only set non-essential cookies, such as analytics, if you agree through the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time using Cookie Settings on the website. The App does not use cookies.

10. Advertising
cherry does not show adverts at the moment. In future we may show adverts from charities to help raise funds and awareness. These will not use your personal data to target you, and we will update this policy before they go live.

11. How we protect your data
Your data is encrypted in transit and stored with providers who meet recognised security standards. Access is limited to the people who need it to run cherry. If a data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will report it to the Information Commissioner's Office within 72 hours and tell you without undue delay where required.

12. Complaints
If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first at cherry.community.interest.company@gmail.com. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and keep you updated.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or by calling 0303 123 1113.

13. Changes to this policy
We will update this policy when the way we use personal data changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. If we make significant changes, we will tell you in the App or by email before they take effect.

This policy is also published on our website so you can read it at any time.
